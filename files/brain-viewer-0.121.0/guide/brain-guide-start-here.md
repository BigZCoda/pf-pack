# Your brain: start here

Your brain is a folder of plain text files on your computer. Your own Claude reads and writes it, so it remembers your work, the people you work with and your projects from one session to the next. The Brain Viewer shows that folder as pages in your browser. Both run on your computer, and the folder is yours.

This guide is short on purpose. Read this page and the day-to-day how-to first, and come back to the rest when you need it.

## Three things to do first

1. **Open your brain.** Open a terminal in your brain folder and type `claude`. The first time, say **set up my brain**. Claude asks your name, what you are working on and how you like information delivered, then sets up the rest with you. It takes about five minutes.
2. **Open the viewer.** Say **start the viewer**, then go to http://127.0.0.1:8765 in your browser. It opens on Today, the page that lists what is yours to do.
3. **Read a day with your brain.** It walks through a morning, a call, a task and the end of the day, with what to say to Claude at each.

You can always get back here from Today: the line about this guide sits at the top of Today until you press **got it**, and at the bottom of Today after that.

## Finding your way in the viewer

Every page has a **?** at the top right. It says what the page is and what each part of it does. The manual, under **more** in the menu, gathers every page's explanation in one place.

## When something doesn't work

Tell Claude what you saw and what you typed. It can read the brain's own instructions and often fix the problem. If it can't, tell ProspectForge.
