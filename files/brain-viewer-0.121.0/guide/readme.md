# skills/brain-viewer/guide/: the guide to a brain, for the person whose brain it is

**What's in here.** The one guide every brain made from the kit comes with, written to the person who uses the brain: `brain-guide-start-here.md` (the start), `brain-guide-a-day.md` (a day with the brain, with what to say to Claude at each point), `brain-guide-part-by-part.md` (Today, people, projects and tasks, calls and transcripts, asking Claude, what stays on the computer) and `brain-guide-what-it-can-do.md` (a short presentation in eight slides).

**Authoritative or derivative.** Authoritative for what a new brain's person is told about their brain. It ships inside the viewer, so every brain that takes the viewer's next update gets it, and Today is its door (one line near the top, under Up first, until the person presses got it, then one line at the foot of the page).

**When to read.** When a person asks how their brain works or what to say to Claude; before changing what the kit does, to keep this guide true.

**When NOT to read.** To learn how the viewer is built (that is the folder's own readme and its code).

**What does NOT belong here.** Anything about one person or one company, anything the kit does not do yet (except the presentation's last slide, which says so), and quotations.

**Naming.** Every file is prefixed `brain-guide-`, so its name is unique in any brain.

**Last touched.** 2026-11-12 (written with viewer 0.121.0).
