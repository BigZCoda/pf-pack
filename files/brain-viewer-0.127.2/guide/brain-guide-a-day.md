# A day with your brain

What a normal day looks like, and what to say to Claude at each point. The lines in bold are examples: say them in your own words, and Claude understands.

[Back to the start](/reader?path=skills%2Fbrain-viewer%2Fguide%2Fbrain-guide-start-here.md)

## In the morning

Open a terminal in your brain folder, type `claude`, and type anything, or `/start-session`. Claude reads what happened in your last session, your standing reminders, what is open on your lists and anything you pressed in the viewer since, then gives you the rundown.

Then open the **today** page and work down it from the top:

- **Up first** holds the one to three things to do or answer now, each with a line on why it is there.
- **Due today** and **New since you last looked** sit under it, then **Also yours**, the next few in the same order.
- Everything else is folded under **Backlog**, and what you put off waits under **On hold** with the day it comes back.

A task has three presses: **done** when it is finished, **hold** to put it off for a few days, and **note** for a line you want to keep with it. A task with steps shows each step with a box to tick. A question has a press for each answer, or a box for your own words. Each press can be undone for the rest of the day.

Today puts everything in one order so you spend the morning on the work, not on deciding what the work is.

Things to say:

- **What's on my plate today?**
- **Remind me every session to check the order list.** It goes into your standing reminders, and every rundown repeats it.

## During the day

When something new comes up, say it to Claude in plain words and it goes where it belongs:

- **Add a task: send the supplier list to the warehouse lead by Friday.**
- **I just learned the warehouse lead wants updates by email. Add it to her card.**
- **What's still open on the returns project?**

When you are away from the terminal, use the **notepad** at the bottom left of any page. Each note is kept as a dated file in your brain. Later, say **read my notes from today and put what belongs on my lists**.

The **reader** opens any file in your brain as a page. Point at a section to **Edit** it, or **Ask** a question about it, which goes onto a project's list with a link back to that spot. The search box at the top of every page finds threads, tasks, people and files.

## A call

**Before it.** Say **what should I know before my call with the warehouse lead?** Claude answers from her card, from the thread the call is about and from your past calls with her. Ask it to **write a short prep**: the questions you want answered and what you want to tell each person. A prep for a call about a matter you follow starts from that matter's thread.

**During it.** Take part as usual, and keep your notes in the notepad. If your employer allows it, have the meeting tool make a transcript. Your brain is built from what was actually said, so the transcript is worth more than your notes. Once your calls show on **today**, each one opens the **call** page: the prep with a box to tick each question as it is asked, and your notes beside it.

**After it.** Save the full transcript as a text file in your brain's `transcripts` folder (the whole transcript, not the short summary some tools make beside it), then say:

- **Process the newest transcript.** Claude writes a summary, the decisions, the action items with their owners, and what the call says about each person on it, and updates their cards.
- **Put my action items from that call on my task list.**
- **Add what this call changed to the returns thread.**
- **What did we decide about the supplier?** Claude answers from the transcript itself.

You type none of it again. The call becomes the record, and the cards, lists and threads are updated from it.

In a new brain, dropping a transcript onto the call page does not read it in yet. Saving it in the folder and saying the line above does.

## When you are given an assignment

Say **start a project** followed by its name. Claude asks for the assignment and walks you through the first steps, each ending in a file. [How your brain holds your work](/reader?path=skills%2Fbrain-viewer%2Fguide%2Fbrain-guide-part-by-part.md) says what the steps are and why.

## When Claude gets something wrong

Correct it and say **remember that**. Claude writes the correction into your brain's rules with the date, and every later session follows it.

## At the end of the day

- **I'm done for today**, or `/done-for-today`. Claude writes down what happened and what is still open, so tomorrow's session starts where this one stopped.
- **Commit and push**, if your brain has a repository. This saves a copy of your brain there.

If anything you pressed in the viewer is still waiting, the **Send** press at the bottom right shows the count. Press it, so your next session reads it first.

The viewer can stay running overnight. To close it, use **Stop the viewer** on the **Settings** page.

## Once a week

Say **check for updates**, or `/update`. Claude shows what is new for your brain and installs it only after your yes. When a newer viewer is ready, **today** also says so in one line.
