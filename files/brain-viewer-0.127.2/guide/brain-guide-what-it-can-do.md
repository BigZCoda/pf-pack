# What your brain can do

A short list of what your brain and the viewer do for you, each with why it helps you move forward. Everything in the first list works in a new brain. The second list says what does not work yet.

## What it does now

- **It remembers your work.** One folder on the computer you work on holds your calls, people, projects and decisions, and Claude reads it before it answers. You never explain the same thing twice, so every session starts where the last one stopped.
- **It puts your day in one order.** The **today** page shows what to do or answer now, what is due and what is new. You spend your attention on the work instead of on sorting it.
- **It turns calls into records.** Say **process the newest transcript** and a call becomes a summary, its decisions, its action items with owners, and updates to the cards of the people on it. What was said is kept, so you can ask about it weeks later.
- **It keeps a card on each person.** Who they are, what they care about and what is open between you, in one place before every conversation.
- **It follows a matter across many calls.** A thread holds where something stands and what was decided, so you pick it up again without starting over.
- **It walks the start of a project with you.** **Start a project** takes you through the purpose, who to talk to, the limits and the tools, each ending in a file and a task, so you build what is needed and not what you guessed.
- **It lets you draw a process before you build.** The **forge** page draws how something works, and its live mode shows what exists against what is only drawn.
- **It keeps a thought the moment you have it.** The **notepad** on every page saves a dated note into your brain.
- **It lets you read and change any file.** The **reader** and **files** pages open every file in your brain, and the search box at the top of every page finds threads, tasks, people and files.
- **It learns how you work.** Correct Claude and say **remember that**, and every later session follows the correction.
- **It brings in what you already have.** **Survey my files** counts what you keep elsewhere on the computer without opening anything; **ingest the next type** brings one kind of it in after your yes. The originals stay where they are.
- **It stays yours.** Everything stays on the computer you work on, and updates install only after your yes.

## Not yet in a new brain

- **Calls on today.** Nothing in a new brain reads your calendar yet, so today shows no calls and no call page opens from it.
- **Claude's pick for Up first.** Up first is filled by the page from your own lists.
- **Sharing.** It is being built, and turns on by invitation once it is ready.
- **The treasure map as a page.** For now it is a way to think about a project.
- **Fixed steps after assess.** Building, testing, delivering and driving a project are done with Claude in your own words.
