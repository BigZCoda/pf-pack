# skills/brain-viewer/guide/: the guide to a brain, for the person whose brain it is

**What's in here.** The one guide every brain made from the kit comes with, written to the person who uses the brain: `brain-guide-start-here.md` (what the brain is for, the two ways to work, the first day in a new brain), `brain-guide-a-day.md` (a day with the brain, with what to say to Claude at each point), `brain-guide-part-by-part.md` (how the brain holds work: projects, threads, people cards, starting a project, the treasure map, the Forge, the Anatomy, sharing, what stays on the computer) and `brain-guide-what-it-can-do.md` (a short list of what it does, and what a new brain does not do yet).

**Authoritative or derivative.** Authoritative for what a new brain's person is told about their brain. It ships inside the viewer, so every brain that takes the viewer's next update gets it, and Today is its door (one line near the top, under Up first, until the person presses got it, then one line at the foot of the page).

**When to read.** When a person asks how their brain works or what to say to Claude; before changing what the kit does, to keep this guide true.

**When NOT to read.** To learn how the viewer is built (that is the folder's own readme and its code).

**What does NOT belong here.** Anything about one person or one company, anything the kit does not do yet (except where a page says plainly that it does not), quotations, and the more abstract ideas behind the brain (those belong in a mindset workbook of their own). Everything here has to be true for a brain built from the kit on its first day.

**Naming.** Every file is prefixed `brain-guide-`, so its name is unique in any brain.

**Last touched.** 2026-11-13 (the four pages rewritten, the why first, from the concept work; true for a new brain on day one). Earlier: 2026-11-12 (written with viewer 0.121.0).
