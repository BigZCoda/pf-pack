# Brain Viewer releases

What each released version of the component changed, newest first. Written by release.py.

## 0.98.0 (2026-10-29)

A task or a thread can be put in front of the person this brain shares a copy with. A task on a list their copy holds carries share with <member> in its presses on Today, the thread page and the workspace, and a thread page whose file goes to them carries the same press under its name. The press puts it on the From band of their Today, under Shared with you, at their next sync: the task read only as it stands on their copy of its list, the thread as one press to its page. Once shared, the card says so with stop sharing. Nothing new crosses: a task on a list that stays home, a task marked private, and a thread whose file is held back draw no press. The owner's sync now runs only from the brain the viewer is installed in. Also in this version (0.97.1): the Manual's chapter list sits inside its card.

## 0.97.1 (2026-10-29)

The Manual's chapter list sits inside its card. The list on the left of the Manual has the same margin inside its frame as the other cards, so the first chapter and the line under the list no longer touch the frame; a long list scrolls inside the card and the card stops above the notepad press. On a phone, Contents unfolds with the same margins.

## 0.97.0 (2026-10-29)

A call's transcript comes in from its page. On a call's page, once the call has ended, The transcript sits right under the call's name: add the transcript takes the plain-text file Gemini makes (or one dropped on the box), files it against that call and its date, and shows what happened and what the drop step printed; once a transcript is filed, run the import chain asks the session to read the call in. Today's card for a call that ended with nothing in the brain carries the same press. A transcript a session pulls from Gemini's email shows on the same page, marked pulled from Gemini. A guest speaking under a display name no card carries is matched to the call prep's one other participant.

## 0.96.0 (2026-10-28)

A member's copy becomes her own brain sharing with the owner's: a save is a save, a file from the owner's brain is shared both ways, a file of hers is hers until Share. Also: a press lands on what it shows; the glossary is a chapter of the manual; From Claude without the rating cards; the chat page without the bubbles.

## 0.95.0 (2026-10-27)

A team copy can run the Studio on its own keys and its own ComfyUI, and its exchange is its inbox: pictures and brought-in items go to the copy's own mine/ folder, which updates never touch; the bring-in press on From Claude; the ComfyUI model files and time limit are settings; the Mac start hint

## 0.93.0 (2026-10-26)

Your answers wait until you send them: a Send press at the bottom right of every page, answers going on their own when you leave the viewer or after five quiet minutes, and a Settings page where the hold is set (0.92.0). The rules for an easy viewer in one place and the first build against them (0.93.0): on a task's steps the box is the only tick and a step that names a place carries an open press; Claude's reply to a note, an answer or a done press is drawn under the item's note apart from your words, the newest first and the older ones folded; on a thread page the other open questions stand under Working on and the next takes the answered one's place; a project's name on the In progress strip opens its own work; the Projects page answers questions and acts on tasks; notes from the notepad ride the send.

## 0.91.0 (2026-10-22)

Changed since you last looked: a band at the top of Today, under the pick, whenever the viewer changed since you last looked (the newest entry's lines, a press into the full page, a Seen press), and the same band naming what landed overnight (a run's delivery note, the night review's audit, the engineer's run); a permanent what's-new link under the header's more fold with a dot while unread.

## 0.90.0 (2026-10-22)

Pictures in a note, a one-line header on the phone, a thread kept to its own pieces, and for a team copy: the From band reads the newer what's-new page (what became of your requests, the sentences on what arrived, each file by its title) and the send press brings in GitHub before it pushes, saying Not sent and why when the newest export clashes with a change committed in the folder.

## 0.89.1 (2026-10-21)

0.89.1, on top of 0.88.0 released earlier today. A link written in an item's words, as a path with a label or a markdown link, is drawn as a link on those words; a page route written in the words or in the item's link list is a real page link that navigates, while a document keeps opening beside the page; the bottom row of every card draws all of the item's links, each by the title of what it opens; and the glossary no longer underlines everyday words inside an item.

## 0.88.0 (2026-10-21)

0.88.0, on top of 0.87.1 released earlier today. In a team member's copy, Today is back in the menu and opens on the From band, named after the brain's owner: the questions and new files from the owner's side, drawn so the member can act on them where they are, with the answer, done and note presses writing requests that travel back to the owner, and a line after each press saying where the request really is (sent, waiting, landed). The propose box's button reads Send in a copy and says the same.

## 0.87.1 (2026-10-21)

From 0.68.0 to 0.87.1. Today opens on the day: Up first (the one to three things picked for you), In progress (the projects you are working in), what is new since you last looked, and one list you can finish; a thread is a workspace with the thing in progress first and the progress on the side; Artifacts has a place to press; every opportunity is on one page by stage and a person's card says who they can reach; a survey and a side-by-side choice open on pages of their own; a task carries tickable steps; the words of the ledger are in the glossary; a Sharing page shows a team member's copy as ticks for folders, files and people cards; and in a team copy the People page lists and opens the shared cards, read only. Also: every page measured under a first-paint budget, every press has a way back, old addresses say where things moved, and the viewer no longer carries the owner's brain inside its code.

## 0.68.0 (2026-10-07)

Viewer 0.68.0: Today is the landing page and the one queue; the menu folds; stable question ids so answers flow back into preps; the day drawing derived; the chat picker; the exclusive port bind and all fifteen tests on scratch folders.

## 0.59.0 (2026-10-02)

The home board's horizon milestones and the flow ring now read from files in your brain (context/horizon.json and context/flow.json) instead of being written into the widgets; with no file they say so and draw nothing. Project labels and order, hidden people and the studio prompt are now viewer settings in viewer/settings.json. No shipped file quotes the owner or carries a personal line, and the release gate fails on either from now on. The call page's word-highlight pattern is fixed. The picture viewer zooms. Today shows one queue of what is yours across every ledger. Cards no longer show raw source or key tags. To update, say check for updates in Claude Code and accept the brain-viewer component.

## 0.56.1 (2026-10-02)

Everything since 0.48.0. Numbered lists count again and a question opens the file it points at (0.48.1). The chat stands alone, pictures drop onto the notepad, and a call's prep shows before its card exists (0.49.0). A card can be tested beside the review page and a rating taken back (0.50.0). Transcript line numbers no longer show anywhere, and the call screen has a must tag (0.50.1); the maps draw the systems not built yet (0.50.2). The image studio can draw on a local graphics card through ComfyUI and redraw a picture you give it (0.51.0, 0.51.1); where ComfyUI is installed is a setting, comfy_dir in viewer/settings.json. A Files page lists every file in the brain in one table (0.52.0). A document link opens in the drawer beside the page on every page (0.53.0), and the Reader shows a document as one page with an outline (0.54.0). Every call to the PF App sends a User-Agent, so the first app read no longer fails with 403. References to tasks and questions read as chips that open a preview in place, and the Questions card has room (0.55.0); three Reader leaks closed (0.55.1). The projects page is one list with the ledgers as filters and what waits on you first, questions with named choices answer with one press, and the ask box is back; the People page opens on this week's calls and who you talk to weekly (0.56.0). Update with python skills/update/install-component.py brain-viewer (or /update); stop a running viewer first.

## 0.48.0 (2026-09-23)

The viewer is now a pf-pack component, replaced whole on update, and what you make lives outside it. Your settings, board layouts, looks and widgets live in viewer/ at the brain root, which no update touches; on first start the viewer creates that folder and copies in any settings, layouts and looks it finds from before. You can write your own widgets in viewer/widgets/: each declares contract: 1, loads after the viewer's own, and cannot take the name of one the viewer ships. A brain with no holon registry or content manifest now shows empty pages instead of errors, and the owner defaults to @me until viewer/settings.json names you. The ledger tool and the log tool the viewer runs on ship with it as the components brain-tasks and brain-log. Install or update with python skills/update/install-component.py brain-viewer (or /update).
